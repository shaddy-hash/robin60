60 at 60 · A Year of Intention

A woman in full motion

Robin has mapped her 60th year as a deliberate journey of self-discovery, guided by a mantra she wrote for herself:

"Inhabiting who I already am. I choose to live it awake and with more honesty, connectedness, fun and care. I release the past to make room for a powerful future. Action creates clarity. I choose where my energy goes."

She has mapped this year across 12 chapters of her life — each one an area she is tending with intention:

Seeking places that will change and inspire her
Us, Intentionally — tending her most important relationship
Becoming more fully herself
Staying connected as her kids become themselves
Acts of care & connection
Letting go & making space
Tending the body she lives in
Friendship & chosen family
Creativity without outcome
Meaning, spirit & inner life
Home as a reflection of who she is now
Looking back, looking ahead

Your words and photos will join her own writing to give the artist a full picture of Robin — as she sees herself, and as the people who love her see her.
