A gift for Robin

She is turning 60 on June 1. Leave her a note. Share a memory. It will become something she keeps forever.

Robin Shaddy is someone who has shown up — fully, generously, and with her whole self — across many chapters and many lives. On June 1, she turns 60.

The people who love Robin are spread across the country and across the years. This page is a way of gathering all of you together. Every note left here — and every photograph shared — will be given to artist Cagney King, who will use them as the foundation for a portrait of Robin.

Not a traditional likeness. A painting that captures who she is, told through the words and images of the people who know her. The finished work will be gifted to Robin — a surprise she won't see coming.

On June 1, a selection of your notes will be read aloud to Robin as part of her celebration. She will hear your voice, even if you can't be there.

How this becomes a painting

1
Your words are read aloud on her birthday. A selection of notes will be shared with Robin as part of her June 1 celebration. She will hear voices from across her life — people who couldn't be in the room.
2
Everything goes to the artist. Cagney King — whose work Robin already loves — will receive every note, every photograph, and Robin's own private journal from her 60th year.
3
A portrait is made. Cagney's abstract portraiture layers texture, text, and emotion. Your words may become part of the paint itself. The finished work will reflect not just Robin's face, but who she is.
4
Robin receives it as a gift. The painting will be gifted to Robin — a surprise that began here, with you.
